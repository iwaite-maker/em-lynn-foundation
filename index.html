A Legacy of Giving

Christine & Eugene Lynn

Two lives, joined in Boca Raton, whose partnership built a university, transformed a hospital, and changed thousands of lives.

Christine and Eugene Lynn built their life together in Boca Raton, a Norwegian born nurse and a Kansas City insurance executive who found in each other, and in this community, a shared belief that giving back was simply what you did. Over more than forty years, together and then in the decades since his passing, that belief grew into one of South Florida's most significant philanthropic legacies. This is the story of the Lynn family.

Two Lives Before Boca Raton

Eugene

Born in Kansas City, Missouri, in 1918, Eugene flew for Trans World Airlines from 1944 to 1947 before entering the insurance business, taking over his uncle's Kansas City company and later serving nearly three decades as president of Universal Underwriters Insurance Company. In 1978 he founded LIG Insurance Agency in Boca Raton, the company that became today's Lynn Insurance Group.

Christine

Trained as a registered and surgical nurse in Oslo, Norway, graduating in 1969, Christine spent the following decade working in Norwegian hospitals and eventually as head nurse aboard a Norwegian cruise ship.

A Meeting at Sea

In 1979, Eugene met Christine aboard that ship. They married on January 19, 1980, and she relocated permanently to Boca Raton to build a life with him.

"If anyone had told me 37 years ago when I was an RN in Oslo, Norway, that my husband and I would have a university named after us, I would have told them, 'You're crazy.'"

Christine E. Lynn, at the 2015 groundbreaking of the Christine E. Lynn University Center

Building What Became Lynn University

  1. 1962

    A small women's college is founded on former farmland in Boca Raton.

  2. 1971

    Facing closure, the college is rescued at the last hour.

  3. 1974

    Eugene's friendship with the school's president begins the family's involvement.

  4. 1978

    The Eugene and Mary Lynn Student Center is dedicated.

  5. 1991

    The university is renamed Lynn University, honoring roughly two decades of the couple's sustained giving.

  6. 1996

    The Eugene M. and Christine E. Lynn Library is dedicated.

  7. 1999

    Eugene passes away after a decade on the university's Board of Trustees.

  8. 2015

    Christine announces a fifteen million dollar gift, the largest single gift in the university's history, with a fifteen million dollar matching challenge behind it.

  9. 2016

    Groundbreaking is held on her seventieth birthday, with about fifteen hundred students, faculty, and staff in attendance.

  10. 2019

    The Christine E. Lynn University Center opens.

Carrying Their Vision Forward

When Eugene died in 1999 after twenty years of marriage, Christine carried their shared vision forward, expanding the family's giving dramatically and stepping into the institutional leadership roles he once held. Today she chairs Lynn University's Board of Trustees, chairs Boca Raton Regional Hospital's Board of Trustees, chairs Lynn Insurance Group, and has served as a Stetson University trustee since 1994. She is the sole trustee of the E.M. Lynn Foundation, the private foundation Eugene established, which has granted tens of millions of dollars across South Florida in the decades since his death, in both their names.

The Good Works

Florida Atlantic University, Nursing

Seed funding in 1979, an endowed chair in 1984, and gifts totaling well over ten million dollars since.

Named result: the Christine E. Lynn College of Nursing, the Christine E. Lynn Eminent Scholar Chair in Nursing, and the Christine E. Lynn Advanced Nursing Simulation Clinic.

Florida Atlantic University, Athletics

A five million dollar gift in 2015.

Named result: the Christine E. Lynn Sports Medicine Center.

Boca Raton Regional Hospital, Baptist Health

Ten million dollars in 2010 for women's health, part of a thirty five million dollar gift in 2018, and ongoing support for cancer and heart care.

Named result: the Christine E. Lynn Women's Health and Wellness Institute, the Christine E. Lynn Heart and Vascular Institute, and the Eugene M. and Christine E. Lynn Cancer Institute, whose new proton therapy facility treated its first patient in August 2026.

The Miami Project to Cure Paralysis, University of Miami

A twenty five million dollar pledge in 2012, inspired by the care Eugene received there for a spinal condition.

Named result: the Christine E. Lynn Human Clinical Trials Initiative and the Christine E. Lynn Rehabilitation Center, which opened in 2020, the largest individual gift in the Miami Project's history.

Stetson University

Ongoing trustee level support since 1994.

Named result: the Eugene M. & Christine Lynn Business Center in DeLand, Florida.

"They were wonderful to him. When you see all the young people in wheelchairs, your heart goes out to them."

Christine E. Lynn, on why she pledged twenty five million dollars to the Miami Project

A Legacy Still Being Written

This is not a story with an ending. It is a partnership that continues to shape lives today, born from two people who believed a nurse from Oslo and an insurance executive from Kansas City could change more lives than either imagined. It continues in every student, patient, and family who benefits from what they built together.
