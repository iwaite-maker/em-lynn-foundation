A Legacy of Giving

E.M. Lynn Foundation

Christine and Eugene Lynn built a university, transformed a hospital, and changed thousands of lives in Boca Raton.

A Norwegian nurse. A Kansas City insurance man. Together in Boca Raton, they shared one belief: giving back was simply what you did. Over forty years, that belief grew into one of South Florida's great philanthropic legacies.

Two Lives Before Boca Raton

Eugene

Born in Kansas City in 1918, Eugene flew for Trans World Airlines, then built a career in insurance. In 1978, he founded the Boca Raton agency that became Lynn Insurance Group.

Christine

Trained as a nurse in Oslo, Norway, Christine worked a decade in Norwegian hospitals, then as head nurse aboard a cruise ship.

A Meeting at Sea

Eugene met Christine aboard that ship in 1979. They married in 1980, and she made Boca Raton home. Her love of the sea never faded. She later owned the yacht Norwegian Queen, named for her homeland.

"If anyone had told me 37 years ago when I was an RN in Oslo, Norway, that my husband and I would have a university named after us, I would have told them, 'You're crazy.'"

Christine E. Lynn, at the 2015 groundbreaking of the Christine E. Lynn University Center

Building What Became Lynn University

  1. 1962

    A small women's college is founded on former farmland in Boca Raton.

  2. 1971

    Facing closure, the college is rescued at the last hour.

  3. 1974

    Eugene's friendship with the school's president begins the family's involvement.

  4. 1978

    The Eugene and Mary Lynn Student Center is dedicated.

  5. 1991

    The university is renamed Lynn University, honoring the family's giving.

  6. 1996

    The Eugene M. and Christine E. Lynn Library is dedicated.

  7. 1999

    Eugene passes away on September 30, after a decade on the university's Board of Trustees.

  8. 2015

    Christine gives fifteen million dollars, the largest gift in the university's history, with a matching challenge behind it.

  9. 2017

    Groundbreaking for the University Center is held on Christine's seventieth birthday, celebrated by fifteen hundred students, faculty, and staff.

  10. 2019

    The Christine E. Lynn University Center opens.

Carrying Their Vision Forward

When Eugene passed away in 1999, Christine carried their vision forward. Today she chairs Lynn University, Boca Raton Regional Hospital, and Lynn Insurance Group, and serves as a Stetson University trustee. As sole trustee of the E.M. Lynn Foundation, she has granted tens of millions of dollars across South Florida, in both their names.

The Good Works

Florida Atlantic University, Nursing

Gifts since 1979 totaling well over ten million dollars.

Named: the Christine E. Lynn College of Nursing and Advanced Nursing Simulation Clinic.

FAU Athletics

Florida Atlantic University, Athletics

A five million dollar gift in 2015.

Named: the Christine E. Lynn Sports Medicine Center.

Boca Raton Regional Hospital, Baptist Health

Over forty five million dollars given since 2010 for women's health, cancer, and heart care.

Named: the Christine E. Lynn Women's Health and Wellness Institute, Heart and Vascular Institute, and Eugene M. and Christine E. Lynn Cancer Institute.

The Miami Project to Cure Paralysis, University of Miami

A twenty five million dollar pledge in 2012, inspired by Eugene's own care there.

Named: the Christine E. Lynn Human Clinical Trials Initiative and Rehabilitation Center, the largest gift in the Miami Project's history.

Stetson University

Stetson University

Ongoing trustee level support since 1994.

Named: the Eugene M. & Christine Lynn Business Center in DeLand, Florida.

"They were wonderful to him. When you see all the young people in wheelchairs, your heart goes out to them."

Christine E. Lynn, on why she pledged twenty five million dollars to the Miami Project
